# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age,
body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Assume good intent while addressing technical and safety concerns directly.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from feedback.
- Respect privacy and do not publish simulator data, logs, or personal information belonging to others.

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, trolling, and publishing private information are not
acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately through the repository maintainer's GitHub profile. Maintainers may
remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and other contributions that violate this policy, and may
temporarily or permanently ban contributors for repeated or severe violations.

This policy is based on the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
